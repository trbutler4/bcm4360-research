# Independent challenge to the BCM4360 synthesis

Date: 2026-10-02. Read-only review of all six available `evidence/*audit.md` reports, their exact pins in `SNAPSHOT.md`, and narrowly selected source/provenance references. No hardware, configuration or source changes. `aleferri-audit.md` was **not yet present** at review time; conclusions about its foundation are provisional pending that report. Additional public issue evidence is explicitly dated and is not silently treated as part of a frozen source tree.

## Verdict: architecture yes, combined-driver prescription not yet

A collaborative **b43/BCMA/mac80211** research direction is better supported than resuming the demonstrated brcmfmac/FullMAC shortcut. But the evidence supports **a staged convergence program**, not “combine these components and the driver is nearly done.” The projects' successful checks are not composable acceptance proofs: one measures real but unreliable traffic, another modeled PHY operations, another parser defenses, another isolated DMA containment, and another a healthy proprietary-driver trace. Their union does not prove any coherent open driver can safely initialize, calibrate, transmit, resume and tear down on the user's Air.

Key pins: gonsolo `1493b8de401ddefef05916ce8b354f6d99d97797`; aleferri `a68a838d8793086ea980b6b71603a6a95623eebd`; xZinio `eb271ace1c609a604202df7e3f8956856514a46f`; massimom `d8d8d7751773413beedab4b3105f319651d87ddc`; OpenBRCM main `06d60d610f4e1cba1f20a188453c8f3ba69357fb`, branch `22e77166f73c39dbf3d827c949403fda26e93bc8`; kimptoc main `7f822c789c7f3fda1aab001f79afb5df81642681`, healthy-trace branch `0ba568addc0c2d167938da8d5ddeb7cb9b91ac35`.

## Challenge the convenient component labels

- **“Gonsolo proves the integration.”** It proves a real host-native networking path is possible on an Air6,1, not robust integration. Latest clean-init success is about21%, autonomous ACKs still malfunction, five-GHz RX is zero and radio debugfs walks can hang the host. The most recent evidence supersedes the README's kernel-regression and five-GHz reception narrative. RX/TX descriptor knowledge is valuable, but inherited restart/MAC-suspend behavior must be reviewed, not blessed by traffic. See `gonsolo-audit.md` §§2-6; pinned [note110](https://github.com/gonsolo/bcm4360-acphy/blob/1493b8de401ddefef05916ce8b354f6d99d97797/notes/110-tx-status-attempt-count-bug-fixed-upload-16x.md#L15-L32), [note112](https://github.com/gonsolo/bcm4360-acphy/blob/1493b8de401ddefef05916ce8b354f6d99d97797/notes/112-srom-rev11-decode.md#L20-L24).
- **“Aleferri is the procedural PHY foundation.”** Plausible research choice, not a current Air operational baseline. Its target is a five-GHz-only DSL-3580L BCM4352/FEM6, not Air FEM2; default2.4GHz returns `-EOPNOTSUPP`, temperatures are not converted, and its README says no recent hardware run. More importantly, the Sept30 independent [issue4](https://github.com/aleferri/b43-ac-wip/issues/4) reports an **8-bit versus48-bit TX gain table truncation**, RF-sequencer polling of the wrong trigger bit, and a MAC-suspend precondition bug, then beacons still failing with txphyerr despite accessor-level matching. Issue targets older commits, but **the8-bit table0x20 write is still visible at the exact audited pin**, [`b43/phy_ac.c:L4104-L4105`](https://github.com/aleferri/b43-ac-wip/blob/a68a838d8793086ea980b6b71603a6a95623eebd/b43/phy_ac.c#L4104-L4105). This is an urgent provenance/width correctness question, not license to apply an issue patch blindly. Independent board report is testimony, not a reproduced RF diagnosis here.
- **“xZinio has calibration except one small math hole.”** The IQCC hole is definite, but not the only gap. Its21 green scenarios restore vendor hardware-model state before stages, replay services, and supply simplified orzero RF readings. The missing Linux platform, data-loader boundary, scheduler/error/signal coverage and unverified five-GHz loopback remain after IQCC is fixed. Farrow extraction whitelist also misses symbols the PHY requests. See `xzinio-audit.md:L45-L76,L94-L96,L205-L225`. Passing more pure vectors does not establish live IQ compensation quality.
- **“Massimom supplies board data.”** It supplies a useful **fail-closed format/parser and board-profile discipline**, not publicly usable Air calibration data. The actual packages and generator are absent, and its sole profile is RT-AC68U passive RX. CRC/shape/range checks prevent many malformed packages, not physically wrong RF values. See `massimom-audit.md:L35-L48,L50-L81`.
- **“OpenBRCM supplies safe access.”** It supplies valuable **invariants and post-loss containment lessons**. Its branch's band-init experiment reset the platform, the formerly asserted stop-before-PHY checkpoint was retired, and the resulting protections were statically tested, not a proof every first access/teardown is safe. In-flight DMA ownership cannot be transferred to b43 just by copying guard helpers. See `openbrcm-branch-audit.md:L46-L56,L75-L91` and `openbrcm-main-audit.md:L48-L54`.
- **“Kimptoc's healthy trace is another BCM4360 Air oracle.”** The capture's PCI ID matches, but internal chip word is4352/package1, versus the Apple4360 word. Its analysis mislabels widths/mapping fields, does not correlate all PCI-window switches and does not establish identical ARM firmware. Useful raw comparator, not same-board ground truth or FullMAC revival. See `kimptoc-audit.md:L310-L344`.

## Best first contribution: choose by objective, do not promise a networking fix

| Candidate | Defensible first deliverable | Main limitation | Ranking |
|---|---|---|---|
| Nonzero IQCC | Pure, hardware-free failing regression vectors and clarified mathematical contract, then independently derived implementation | No evidence this fixes gonsolo's TX initialization or ACK failure; RX IQ path is not automatically the TX fault | **Best bounded first external patch** |
| Width-aware raw trace decoder | Narrow48-bit table fixture proving full data-port coverage, loss/unknown classification, then typed cross-format decoder | Full core-aware decode cannot infer absent PCI-window switches; collecting missing data is separate work | **Highest cross-project information/safety leverage**, worth prioritizing if contributing to convergence infrastructure |
| Board SPROM | Exact Apple rev11 fixture/provenance and consumer contract, crosschecked against existing extractors | Several working decoders already exist; synthetic identity or ASUS data does not supply actual Apple analog calibration | Third unless exact Air-board mismatch is shown to dominate failures |

**Recommended initial scope:** submit **nonzero IQCC tests plus a precise issue/contract correction** before claiming an implementation finished. Existing audit has five large nonzero mismatches and a small zero-angle mismatch against a pinned vendor helper. Add equal/unequal powers, signed cross-power, zero denominators, saturation and physically feasible covariance cases (`IQ² <= I2*Q2`), separate invalid-input handling, fixed-point rounding and shift/overflow tests. Independently derive phase/amplitude physics, do not mechanically transcribe decompiler structure. See [IQCC stub](https://github.com/xZinio/bcm4360/blob/eb271ace1c609a604202df7e3f8956856514a46f/open/phy/phy_cal.c#L1233-L1250) and [documented contract](https://github.com/xZinio/bcm4360/blob/eb271ace1c609a604202df7e3f8956856514a46f/docs/re/spec/acphy-cal-rx.md#L276-L296). Acceptance: failing tests at this pin, independent expected mathematics plus licensed oracle agreement, sanitizer/edge checks, and no existing21-scenario regressions. **Not acceptance:** one helper equal to the object, therefore calibration/RF ready.

If the primary goal is **integration progress rather than a small safe first patch**, promote the width-aware **fixture-first decoder** above IQCC. Issue4's wide-table loss and kimptoc's width/window confusion show that green logical comparisons can hide materially unsafe bus differences. Preserve access width, complete multiword table entries, ordering/auto-increment, core/window source identity, read-return pairing, loss markers and unknown state. Never collapse unobserved reads/direct stores into “matched.” Use a specifically scoped fixture for48-bit table0x20 rather than attempting all vendor formats at once. Do not export real network/MAC identifiers. No automatic hardware capture belongs in its host tests.

## Underestimated integration, security and licensing costs

1. **One lifecycle owner is required.** b43 counters/locks/workqueues, mac80211 callbacks, PHY calibration windows and DMA teardown have implicit contracts. Issue4's worker entered with MAC running while the implementation expected suspended illustrates why trace-matched routines can be wrongly integrated. Define save/restore, asynchronous work cancellation, device-loss latching, buffer ownership and suspend failure postconditions before combining implementations.
2. **Kernel/RF safety is not host-test success.** A bad PCIe read may be all-ones onx86 or synchronous external abort onARM64 (issue4). Device-loss guards are not a sandbox. Failing closed must cover data/board mismatch, unfinished RF sequencing, invalid calibration results and unresolved DMA ownership. Receive-only `NO_IR`/noTX policies should not be weakened merely because association becomes the next milestone.
3. **Crypto/status surfaces are attack surfaces.** Firmware-controlled key indexes, RX sizes/metadata, aggregation reassembly and TX status must have explicit bounds/error semantics. Massimom's key-index bounds backport is valuable, not proof the other AC receive parsers are safe. Firmware CRC/hash identity is neither provenance permission nor emitted-power validation.
4. **No unified redistributable data supply exists.** Same vendor version/object hash does not make tables interchangeable across board/chip/radio/microcode revisions. xZinio uses direct-symbol data inemulation while deployment whitelist omits Farrow, massimom omits board packages, and gonsolo carries literal extracted values. A per-symbol dependency manifest, exact input hashes, endian/width schema and board applicability contract are prerequisite infrastructure.
5. **Do not accidentally merge provenance channels.** Gonsolo explicitly studied decompiled proprietary code; OpenBRCM/xZinio claim separated specification processes with caveats. A contributor reading every proprietary-derived implementation cannot then claim they followed a “never saw implementation” clean-room role. Delegate reviewed behavior specs independently and preserve provenance/access records. Legal review is still required.
6. **GPL labels require a per-file matrix.** Aleferri's top-levelLICENSE isGPLv3, while `b43/phy_ac.c`, xmit and many others explicitly useGPL2-or-later, and `ppr_ac.c` usesGPL2. Therefore blanket “GPL3 incompatible” is too crude, but blanket “allGPL so mergeable” is wrong too. Confirm each new file/patch/tool's applicable terms, permissions and upstream notices. Its [PROVENANCE:L38-L51](https://github.com/aleferri/b43-ac-wip/blob/a68a838d8793086ea980b6b71603a6a95623eebd/PROVENANCE.md#L38-L51) leavesAGSOT reacquisition unresolved and MacBook capture redistribution permission pending. A GPL firmware source archive can still contain proprietary wl objects; public availability does not itself license redistribution.

## Evidence that would change this recommendation

- **Choose an implementation baseline confidently:** repeat real managed WPA2/DHCP, interface-isolated bidirectional traffic, AP retry/ACK counters, cold/reload/resume cohorts and an independent second Apple board, with exact firmware/module/table hashes and externally observed RF behavior. This would outweigh synthetic match percentages.
- **PrioritizeSPROM overIQCC/decoder:** a controlled target-board parameter correction reproducibly removes failures while kernel/source/RF environment remain fixed, or a proved extractor error feeds wrongFEM/PA/chain data into active logic. Printing corrected values alone is insufficient.
- **PrioritizeIQCC as an operational fix:** reproduce the nonzero coefficients in the actual calibration path and demonstrate RF/traffic improvement after correcting only that arithmetic. Today this causality is not shown.
- **Treat proceduralPHY as safe enough to port:** show the wide-table/sequence/window/worker preconditions repaired at a new explicit pin, raw bus coverage beyond accessor calls, and stable beacon+TX/RX evidence on target hardware. Hundreds of equal PHY operations do not meet this threshold.
- **Reconsiderbrcmfmac:** lawful target-appropriate firmware must demonstrate control transactions **and asynchronousWLAN events and packets**, plus safe DMA/lifecycle. ARM console publication/PCI-CDC strings or a healthywltrace are not enough.

**Bottom line:** keep collaborative SoftMAC as the direction, but recommend **a first bounded correctness contribution, not a user driver swap or copy-and-paste mega-port**. NonzeroIQCC regressions are the cleanest safe entry point; width-correct raw-trace fixtures are the strongest cross-project leverage; exact AirSPROM work becomes first only with target-specific causal evidence. No new hardware or numeric experiments were run for this challenge.

## Updated review after aleferri audit: evidence gates first

**Update: 2026-10-02, 15:30 UTC. This section supersedes the provisional contribution ranking above.** `aleferri-audit.md` is now available and was reviewed alongside the coordinator's `REPORT.md` draft. The original six-report review and its chronology are preserved, but aleferri is no longer absent from this assessment. No further tests, hardware accesses, source changes or configuration changes were performed in this follow-up. This evidence-document append was subsequently requested by the coordinator.

### Revised ordering, separated by objective

1. **Safest and quickest broadly useful first patch: comparator regression witnesses plus narrowly scoped fail-closed fixes.** Begin with empty state never receiving authoritative `IDENTICAL/0`, asymmetric overlay tests, corrected wrapper A/B association, and preserved OBJ selectors. These defects have reproduced host-only witnesses and crisp acceptance checks. Do not bundle a full D11 simulator, universal trace decoder or complete template-memory model into that initial patch.
2. **Highest-leverage project evidence infrastructure: width-complete, window-aware raw capture reconciliation.** Preserve selectors, access widths, full multiword table data, ordering, source identity and loss/unknown markers. Correct comparison semantics before trusting higher match scores. A decoder cannot recover omitted PCI-window transitions, lost high words or asynchronous events that the capture never recorded.
3. **Best separable upstream hardware-enabling contribution: bind rev-11 SROM tests to the actual kernel extractor.** Test `bcma_sprom_extract_r11`, not a driftable copy, against independently sourced raw vectors, nonzero sub-band fields, inactive-core sentinels and older-revision regressions. This deserves priority without proving that SPROM causes the present Air failure: it repairs an existing verification boundary. Actual board provisioning remains a separate applicability/provenance problem.
4. **Best bounded numerical RE task for the user's mathematical interest: nonzero RX-IQCC.** The known stub and oracle mismatches remain excellent independent work, but IQCC is not the highest-leverage prerequisite for overall project progress. There is still no demonstrated causal connection to gonsolo's intermittent initialization, ACK trouble or latest zero 5 GHz RX. It may proceed in parallel after establishing an independent mathematical contract and appropriate provenance roles.

### New evidence driving the revision

The following are audit-reproduced host-side defects, not hypothetical RF diagnoses. Source links use aleferri's exact pin `a68a838d8793086ea980b6b71603a6a95623eebd`:

- **Selector alias:** [`reverse-tools/tracelib.py:62-72`](https://github.com/aleferri/b43-ac-wip/blob/a68a838d8793086ea980b6b71603a6a95623eebd/reverse-tools/tracelib.py#L62-L72) removes `sel=`, allowing distinct OBJ address spaces to compare equal. See `aleferri-audit.md` §7.3.
- **False raw equality through tolerance:** bus comparison calls value-tolerant equality at [`test/unit/cmp_skip.py:402-410`](https://github.com/aleferri/b43-ac-wip/blob/a68a838d8793086ea980b6b71603a6a95623eebd/test/unit/cmp_skip.py#L402-L410) and `435-437`. The audit's 17-operation fixture scored strict non-bus 16/18 but bus 17/17 after a one-LSB change. Raw equality and permitted numerical tolerance must be distinct verdicts.
- **Empty comparison surface:** empty streams produce empty state dumps and `compare_state.py` returns `IDENTICAL/0`. Successful parsing or two empty outputs is not coverage. See audit §8.2.
- **Reversed microcode ownership:** [`test/d11sim/run_init_equiv.sh:60-74`](https://github.com/aleferri/b43-ac-wip/blob/a68a838d8793086ea980b6b71603a6a95623eebd/test/d11sim/run_init_equiv.sh#L60-L74) associates wl with `--ucode-a` and b43 with `--ucode-b`, then compares A=b43/B=wl. Correct the associations and add asymmetric witnesses.
- **Unilateral overlay hides opposite-side mismatch:** [`test/d11sim/compare_state.py:69-80`](https://github.com/aleferri/b43-ac-wip/blob/a68a838d8793086ea980b6b71603a6a95623eebd/test/d11sim/compare_state.py#L69-L80) unions overlay keys and excludes those cells on both sides. A real host SHM difference changed from `DIFFERENT/1` to `IDENTICAL/0` with an overlay supplied only on A. Track each side's ownership and chronological last writer rather than blanket exclusions.
- **Beacon payload differences disappear:** the C D11 model has no template-memory array. Different first RAM_DATA words followed by the same final word yield identical snapshots. See [`test/d11sim/d11_core.h:63-79`](https://github.com/aleferri/b43-ac-wip/blob/a68a838d8793086ea980b6b71603a6a95623eebd/test/d11sim/d11_core.h#L63-L79), `d11_core.c:85-96`, `snapshot.c:3-37`, and audit §8.2. Either model this class or declare the verdict incomplete for beacon correctness.
- **Passing SROM tests do not test the production function:** [`bcma/harness/extract_r11.c:1-36`](https://github.com/aleferri/b43-ac-wip/blob/a68a838d8793086ea980b6b71603a6a95623eebd/bcma/harness/extract_r11.c#L1-L36) is a separate corrected draft parser, while the production function is [`bcma/drivers/bcma/sprom.c:486`](https://github.com/aleferri/b43-ac-wip/blob/a68a838d8793086ea980b6b71603a6a95623eebd/bcma/drivers/bcma/sprom.c#L486). [`bcma/README.md:92-103`](https://github.com/aleferri/b43-ac-wip/blob/a68a838d8793086ea980b6b71603a6a95623eebd/bcma/README.md#L92-L103) acknowledges the separation. The reproduced 77/82 raw-board PASS counts cannot certify production extraction. See audit §11.1.

Independent issue #4 strengthens the width-aware case, but remains third-party testimony about older commits and a different board. Accessor matching missed an 8-versus-48-bit TX table issue, and local fixes still did not produce visible beacons. Thus width-correct input is necessary, not sufficient for RF acceptance. Safety work must also propagate unsupported-FEM/calibration failures, respect MAC-suspend/work ownership, and stop advertising HT/VHT RX beyond verified parser behavior. See `aleferri-audit.md` §§4.3, 5.2, 6.3 and 15.

### Conditions that would change the revised ordering

An independently strict capture/test path proving these comparator defects irrelevant to the intended workflow could lower their priority. Actual-source extractor tests already covering raw nonzero Air data and older revisions could close the SROM gate. Real measured IQ accumulators plus a controlled RF experiment attributing target failure to IQCC could elevate that arithmetic to an operational priority. None is established by the current reports.

The collaborative b43 direction remains an architectural recommendation, not a claim the repositories compose. Capture redistribution permission, external interpreter/tool pins and file-level licensing remain unresolved. Existing vendor-derived knowledge also constrains what a contributor can truthfully claim about clean-room role separation. **Updated bottom line: repair demonstrably false-positive evidence gates first, validate the actual extractor and capture semantics next, and present IQCC as a parallel bounded numerical research option rather than the universal first project fix.**
